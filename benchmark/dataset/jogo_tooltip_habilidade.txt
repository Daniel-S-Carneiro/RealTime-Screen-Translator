Dream of Realms Lv.5
Makes self enter the [Sleep] status for 4 turn(s), revives all fallen allies, then grants all allies 2 turns of [Shield] and [DEF Boost]
[Sleep] Cannot act; but reduces duration by 1 turn when taking DMG
[Revive] Revives fallen allies and restores 20% HP
[Shield] Negates part of incoming DMG; the negated DMG by the shield equals 14% of HP
[DEF Boost] Increases DEF by 48%
CD: 8 turns
